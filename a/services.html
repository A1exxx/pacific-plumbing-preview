ITEM 01 / 05 Our specialty

Whole-home repiping

Old galvanized, polybutylene or leaking copper supply lines replaced with PEX or copper, start to finish.

What we do

  1. a.Walk-through of the house and a written quote
  2. b.New supply lines in PEX or copper
  3. c.New shut-off valves
  4. d.Pressure test before walls are closed

Signs you need it

  • Rusty or discolored water
  • Low pressure on several fixtures
  • Pinhole leaks that keep coming back
  • Original pipes from before 1990
