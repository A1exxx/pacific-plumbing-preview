What we can fix for you

Whole-home repiping

If your home still has original galvanized, polybutylene or corroded copper pipes, patching one leak at a time gets expensive. A repipe replaces the supply lines in one planned job.

What is included

  • Walk-through and a fixed written quote
  • New PEX or copper supply lines
  • New shut-off valves at fixtures
  • Pressure test before walls are closed

Signs it is time

  • Rusty or discolored water
  • Low pressure on several fixtures
  • Repeat pinhole leaks
  • Home with original pre-1990 pipes

Drain cleaning

Kitchen sinks, showers, tubs, toilets and main lines. We clear the clog properly instead of pouring chemicals down the pipe.

What we do

  • Clear sink, tub and shower drains
  • Main line and sewer backups
  • Tell you why it clogged and how to prevent it

Call us if you notice

  • Water draining slowly
  • Gurgling sounds
  • Sewer smell or water coming back up

Leak detection & repair

A hidden leak can rot a floor or grow mold for months before you see it. We track it down, open only what is needed, and fix it.

What we do

  • Find leaks in walls, ceilings and floors
  • Slab and under-floor leaks
  • Repair the pipe and test the line

Call us if you notice

  • A water bill higher than usual
  • Damp spots, stains or a musty smell
  • Sound of running water when taps are off
